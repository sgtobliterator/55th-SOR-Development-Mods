[h1]FORGES OF DORN: Zeus Additions[/h1]
[i]Zeus & 3DEN Module Expansion[/i]

[b]Forges of Dorn[/b] is a collection of streamlined tools designed to automate AI behavior, spawning, and mission flow. It aims to improve the Zeus experience by reducing the workload during large-scale operations—minimizing repetitive manual setup and the need for constant micro-management.

[b]Now updated with Vanilla & CBA fallbacks[/b], allowing core modules to function without requiring LAMBS as a hard dependency.

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Spawn AI Group[/b] (Zeus & 3DEN) - Advanced spawning with [VANILLA], [CBA], or [LAMBS] tasks. Supports faction/group classnames and quoted/unquoted strings.
[*] [b]Garrison Area[/b] (Zeus) - Instantly occupy buildings. includes a vanilla fallback that uses Hold/Suppress logic and scalable area patrols, height filters and more.
[*] [b]Defense OP Generator[/b] (Zeus & 3DEN) - Create infinite wave-defense scenarios with directional arcs and Line-of-Sight spawn checks.
[*] [b]FOD Tasks[/b] (Zeus) - Context menu and waypoint types for LAMBS (Ambush, Hit & Run, QRF). Now loads dynamically only when LAMBS is present.
[*] [b]Utility Tools[/b] - Vehicle Garage (3DEN/Zeus), Weapon Add/Remove, and Faction Variable Logger.
[/list]

[h2]Spawn AI Group (3DEN & Zeus)[/h2]
Spawn fully configured groups with a single click or trigger. The UI now features Prefix Tags ([LAMBS], [CBA], [VANILLA]) to clearly show which tasks require specific dependencies.

[b]New Flexibility:[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Smart Input:[/b] Enter unit classnames with or without quotes (e.g., [i]O_Soldier_F[/i] or [i]"O_Soldier_F"[/i]).
[*] [b]Faction & Group Support:[/b] (3DEN) Define a [b]Faction Classname[/b] or [b]CfgGroups entry[/b] (e.g., [i]OIA_InfSentry[/i]) to automate unit selection.
[*] [b]Expanded Tasks:[/b] Includes new [b]Vanilla[/b] (Sentry, Guard, Seek & Destroy) and [b]CBA[/b] (Attack, Defend, Patrol, Search) behaviors.
[/list]

[h2]Garrison Area[/h2]
Populate a zone with AI defenders instantly.
[list]
[*] [b]LAMBS Version:[/b] Uses native LAMBS Garrison logic for dynamic building defense.
[*] [b]Vanilla Fallback:[/b] Automatically active if LAMBS is missing. Units occupy buildings with [b]Hold/Suppress[/b] logic, disabling pathing until engaged.
[*] [b]Area Patrols:[/b] Scalable patrol elements (1-4 units) will walk perimeter routes to secure the objective.
[/list]

[h2]Defense OP Generator[/h2]
Continuously spawns AI waves attacking a fixed position.
[list]
[*] [b]Zeus:[/b] Use the multi-step dialog to set faction, radius, and interval. Delete the [b]VR Selector[/b] to stop the assault.
[*] [b]3DEN:[/b] Pre-configure attributes and activate via trigger. Supports [b]Max Duration[/b] or custom [b]SQF Stop Conditions[/b].
[*] [b]Features:[/b] Supports directional attacks (sector arcs), Line-of-Sight checks (spawning behind cover), and "Players Only" targeting.
[/list]

[h2]FOD Tasks (ZEN Waypoints)[/h2]
Custom tasks integrated into the ZEN context menu and waypoint list. Note: This module now uses an optional config and will only load if [b]LAMBS_WP[/b] is detected.
[list]
[*] [b]Setup Ambush:[/b] Move to cover, go silent, and wait for contact.
[*] [b]Hit & Run:[/b] Engage briefly, retreat, and reposition—repeat indefinitely.
[*] [b]Reserve/QRF:[/b] Hold position until enemies are detected nearby, then rush to assist.
[/list]

[i]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1858075458]LAMBS[/url] for FULL usage, but works fine without.

[h2]"Log Faction Variables" - 3DEN Editor Tool[/h2]
A small quality-of-life tool for scenario builders. Select any unit in the 3DEN editor, right-click and choose [b]Log Faction Variables (FOD_ZA)[/b] under the [b]Log[/b] submenu. The faction classname and every group classname from that faction's CfgGroups entry are copied straight to your clipboard, one per line - ready to paste into module fields or scripts.

[b]LOTS OF SETTINGS![/b]
The mod is very configurable as per our design philosophy, this can all be found in the CBA_A3 provided Addon settings.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q:[/b] Does the Vehicle Garage have a Whitelist/Blacklist?
[b]A:[/b] Yes, you can block vehicles in a bunch of ways (side, classnames, etc.). Instead of hiding them, the vehicles stay in the list but are unspawnable (greyed out). You can however 'remove' an entire vehicle category.
